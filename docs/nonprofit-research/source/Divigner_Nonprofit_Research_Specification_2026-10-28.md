## Mission and commercial parameters
- Product: **24/7 Community Engagement Concierge**. Lead with resident access, multilingual navigation, after-hours information, and staff time saved; explain AI concretely with a live demo.
- Price: **$4,500 implementation**, **$300/month** including **four hours of conversation**. Optional capabilities priced separately; do not invent rates.
- Target: North Jersey, with Montclair as sales home base and practical in-person driving distance. Prioritize Essex and adjacent counties.
- Financial screen: latest available annual revenue **at least $1.5 million**, **no ceiling**; note multi-year trends and exceptions on a watchlist. Prefer **10+ employees**, multiple programs/sites, stable funding, complex public inquiries, identifiable decision makers.
- Initial universe: 23 named organizations below. New Community Corporation, Urban League of Essex County, and La Casa de Don Pedro are **existing relationships, not cold-outreach prospects**. Remaining 20 are preliminary, not yet verified as qualified.
- Founder-led, individualized outreach only: personal Divigner business email and LinkedIn; no phone calls, mass campaigns, or automated sending without separate approval.
- Mention the founder's current work with New Community Corporation **generally**, accurately, without claiming endorsement or revealing confidential project details.
- All 20 prospective nonprofits may receive a **separate live interactive demo** using public information. Mark each demo an **independent Divigner prototype, not endorsed or operated by the nonprofit**.
- Demos: suggested 2–3 minute visitor limit, privacy notice, avoid personal/confidential data, track interaction transcripts by dedicated avatar. Do not equate interaction with a specific executive. Consider deactivation after ~30 days of no engagement, while archiving configuration.

## Research sources and verification protocol
1. Confirm exact legal name, EIN, location, and any DBA; avoid confusing similarly named organizations.
2. Obtain latest **IRS Form 990** (federal return) from IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, Candid, or the nonprofit's own disclosures. Record **direct filing URL, source URL, tax year, fiscal year end, access date**, and whether it is a full return, 990-EZ, or other form. Do not invent an IRS filing or URL. Link earlier years when useful.
3. Capture total revenue, expenses, surplus/deficit, assets, liabilities, program service revenue, grants/contributions, other revenue, program spending, management/general spending, fundraising, reported employees/volunteers, leadership and public compensation where available. Note missing or non-comparable fields.
4. Validate against official website, annual reports, program pages, public directories, leadership/team pages, and credible current sources. For every material claim include its **URL and date/filing year**.
5. Document program names, descriptions, eligibility, populations, service area, locations, languages, hours, enrollment/scheduling/intake processes, website navigation friction, and after-hours information gaps. Do not assert call volumes or operational pain without evidence.
6. Find **official** website and social handles/URLs for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X/Twitter, TikTok and others where present. Mark 'not found / unverified' rather than guess. Note activity date when available.
7. Identify executive director/CEO, COO, relevant program or engagement leader, public professional contact routes, and LinkedIn profile only when confidently matched. No speculative private emails or personal data.
8. Assess financial health, operational fit, accessibility needs, local travel practicality, implementation readiness, and strategic/referral potential. Distinguish **verified**, **inferred**, and **unknown**.
9. Rank prospects with a transparent scoring rubric, e.g. 0–5 for financial capacity, engagement need, program complexity, readiness, proximity, and strategic value. Explain score evidence and gaps; do not present inferred scores as facts.
10. Provide a tailored value proposition, outreach hook, 3 representative questions for the live avatar, suggested public-content knowledge base, privacy/accuracy safeguards, and a concise initial email and LinkedIn connection message for each **outreach-eligible** organization.
11. For live demo tracking, record nonprofit, avatar ID, demo URL, creation date, email/LinkedIn dates, transcript activity count and engagement tier, follow-up date, and retirement/archive status. Do not assume page opens from transcript records alone.
12. Research all 23 before declaring a final qualification count. Verify information at research time; mark missing fields explicitly and retain exact citations.
