# Divigner — 20 North Jersey Nonprofit Prospect Profiles
**Research snapshot:** October 28, 2026  
**Status:** Actionable **research starter / partial verification**, not a completed due-diligence database. All 20 profiles included; unverified facts are explicitly identified. Do not import or send automatically.

## Operating rules
- Minimum annual revenue $1.5 million, no upper cap; preferred 10+ employees. These criteria have **not** been verified for every organization.
- Founder-led, one-to-one email from **jae@divigner.com** and personal LinkedIn. No phone calls, mass outreach, or automatic CRM creation.
- Three established relationships (New Community Corporation, Urban League of Essex County, La Casa de Don Pedro) are intentionally excluded from this 20-prospect list.
- $4,500 setup; $300/month for four conversational hours. First outreach leads with access to services, not pricing.
- Individual live, clearly labeled **independent Divigner prototype** for each organization; public content only. Disclose transcript collection; do not solicit confidential or personal data.
- **Verified source** means the linked organization page or 990 directory was located during this research pass. **Working hypothesis** is an untested sales inference. Missing data is not zero.
- Source URLs are provided for validation; the direct Form 990 filing PDF, full staff counts, and verified social handles require a separate extraction pass where not explicitly supplied.
- Some names/web domains are **candidate matches** and must be confirmed before use; verify EIN to prevent conflation.

## Research source hubs
- IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search: https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/tax-exempt-organization-search
- ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer: https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/
- Candid: https://candid.org/

## Preliminary order of approach
Prioritize nearby Essex/Passaic organizations with complex public programs: Ironbound, United Community Corporation, Greater Newark Conservancy, HOPES, NJCDC, Oasis, Eva's Village, CUMAC. This is **editorial triage**, not a verified numerical ranking. HomeFront is geographically outside the primary North Jersey focus and should be considered secondary.
