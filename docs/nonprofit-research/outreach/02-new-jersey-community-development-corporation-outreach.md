**Featured programs (2 to 3, official links)**

1. **Lead-Safe Program** (https://www.njcdc.org/news-1/leadsafe; promoted in the homepage top banner as "100% FREE Lead-Safe Repairs for Paterson Tenants and Landlords"). Qualifying tenants and landlords get free lead testing and repairs by licensed professionals, for single- and multi-family homes and buildings of up to 10 units; most jobs finish in about a week. Booking online (Calendly), by phone 973-413-1709, or by email leadsafe@njcdc.org. Page is in English and Spanish (posted 2026-03-17).
2. **Affordable & Supportive Housing** (https://www.njcdc.org/affordable-supportive-housing). More than 200 units across eight properties. Barclay Place (1, 2 and 3 bedrooms): NJCDC is **not accepting applications** for the waiting list; applications submitted Feb 26 to Mar 19, 2025 are under review; questions to barclay@njcdc.org.
3. **Neighborhood Help Center and Paterson Financial Empowerment Center** (https://www.njcdc.org/community-building). The Help Center is a one-stop shop for housing, education, immigration and other questions; the Financial Empowerment Center offers free one-on-one financial counseling to all Paterson residents. Contact: Director of Community Outreach, 973.413.1641, or the message form.

**Q&A scenario 1**
- Visitor question: "I rent an old house in Paterson. How do I get the free lead test?"
- Grounded answer: "NJCDC's Lead-Safe Program offers free lead testing and repairs to qualifying tenants and landlords, done by licensed professionals. It covers homes and buildings of up to 10 units. You can book online, call, or email the Lead-Safe team. NJCDC decides who qualifies."
- Source: https://www.njcdc.org/news-1/leadsafe
- Safe next step: Book at https://calendly.com/nsnyder-njcdc/lead-test-appointment-request, call 973-413-1709, or email leadsafe@njcdc.org.

**Q&A scenario 2**
- Visitor question: "Can I apply for an apartment at Barclay Place?"

- Source: https://www.njcdc.org/affordable-supportive-housing
- Safe next step: Email the property manager at barclay@njcdc.org with questions.
