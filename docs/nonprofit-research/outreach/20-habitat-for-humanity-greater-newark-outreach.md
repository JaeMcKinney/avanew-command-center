**Featured programs (2 to 3, official links)**

1. **Homeownership Program** (https://www.habitatnewark.org/programs/homeownership-program/). First-time homebuyers buy homes Habitat renovates or builds, sold at or below cost. Orientation is the first step. Three criteria: need for housing, ability to be a responsible homeowner (monthly mortgage and home costs), and willingness to partner, including at least 200 sweat-equity hours. Waitlist through the Fast Track Questionnaire (PDF); qualification chart on the page. Contact: Family Services Coordinator, 973-624-3330 x103.
2. **West & Central Village Community Resource Hub** (https://www.habitatnewark.org/programs/community-resource-hub-programs/). 298 South Orange Ave. Food pantry every Wednesday (tickets at 9:00 AM, first come first served; distribution at 12:00 PM); ID Restoration Program (more than 3,000 documents); computer lab Wednesday and Thursday, 9 AM to 5 PM; health and wellness with University Hospital; referral services line 973-342-2697. Spanish-language contact named on the page.
3. **Critical Repair & Free Smoke Alarm Installation** (https://www.habitatnewark.org/programs/critical-repairs/). Health and safety repairs for low-to-moderate income homeowners (generally at or below 80% AMI). **Not currently accepting new applications**; waitlist through Family Services. Free smoke alarms for homeowners and buildings of four units or fewer (with landlord permission).

**Q&A scenario 1**
- Visitor question: "How do I apply to buy a Habitat home?"
- Grounded answer: "Habitat's homeownership program is for first-time homebuyers. The first step is an orientation. Habitat looks at three things: your need for housing, your ability to be a responsible homeowner, and your willingness to partner, which includes at least 200 hours of sweat equity. To join the waitlist, Habitat asks you to complete the Fast Track Questionnaire."
- Source: https://www.habitatnewark.org/programs/homeownership-program/
- Safe next step: Open the Fast Track Questionnaire from that page and call Family Services at 973-624-3330 x103.

**Q&A scenario 2**
- Visitor question: "My roof leaks and I'm a senior homeowner. Can Habitat fix it?"
- Grounded answer: "Habitat's Critical Repair Program helps low-to-moderate income homeowners with health and safety repairs, but the page says it is not currently accepting new applications. You can ask to be placed on the waitlist. Habitat also installs free smoke alarms."
- Source: https://www.habitatnewark.org/programs/critical-repairs/
- Safe next step: Call Family Services at 973-624-3330 x103 to ask about the waitlist. For an emergency such as a gas leak or fire, call 911.

**Q&A scenario 3**
- Visitor question: "¿Cuándo es la despensa de comida del Hub?" (When is the Hub food pantry?)

Tickets are given out at 9:00 AM, first come first served, and food distribution starts at 12:00 PM. For Spanish, the Hub page names a Spanish-language contact at 973-624-3330, ext. 103."
- Source: https://www.habitatnewark.org/programs/community-resource-hub-programs/
- Safe next step: Contact the Hub coordinator listed on the page, or the Spanish-language contact at ext. 103.
