### Three Q&A scenarios

**1. Visitor:** "I have nowhere to stay tonight in Morris County. What do I do?"

**Web concierge:** Family Promise of Morris County asks anyone experiencing homelessness or a housing crisis to dial 2-1-1, or text your zip code to 898-211. If you're in danger, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988. During the day, the Our Promise Drop-In Center at 51 Washington St in Morristown is open Monday to Friday, 11:00am to 3:00pm. It's one of the county's Coordinated Entry access points.

**Source:** https://www.familypromisemorris.org/contact-us

**Safe next step:** Show 2-1-1 and the text option first. The concierge never says whether someone qualifies for shelter.

**2. Visitor:** "What can I do at the Our Promise drop-in?"

**Web concierge:** Our Promise offers phone and computer access, a mailing address, restrooms, a warming and cooling center, information and referrals, and links to medical, mental health and dental services, plus case management. It's at 51 Washington St, Morristown, open for drop-in Monday to Friday, 11am to 3pm. Outside those hours, you can call 973-644-0100, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

**Source:** https://www.familypromisemorris.org/outreach-programs

**Safe next step:** Give the address, hours and phone, plus OurPromise@FamilyPromiseMorris.org.

**3. Visitor:** "Where will the Navigating Hope van be this week?"

**Web concierge:** Navigating Hope is a mobile outreach van run by Family Promise and Morris County Human Services. It visits all 39 Morris County municipalities at least five days a week. Its current stops are on the county's Navigating Hope calendar, and I'll link you there so you see the latest schedule.

**Source:** https://www.morriscountynj.gov/Departments/Human-Services/Navigating-Hope/Navigating-Hope-Calendar

**Safe next step:** Link the county calendar. The concierge never guesses a stop or a time.

### Languages (verified only)

English only. The site has no translation widget or Spanish pages, so the demo makes no multilingual claim.

### Privacy, safety and accuracy guardrails

- 2-1-1 / text 898-211 first for anyone in a housing crisis; 911 for danger; 988 for mental health crises.
- No shelter, voucher or Coordinated Entry eligibility decisions; never says a bed or voucher is available.
- Collects no names, locations or case details; tells visitors not to share them.
- Van stops come only from the county calendar link, never generated.
- No legal or medical advice.
- Disclaimer on screen: independent Divigner demonstration using publicly available information; not an official Family Promise of Morris County service.
