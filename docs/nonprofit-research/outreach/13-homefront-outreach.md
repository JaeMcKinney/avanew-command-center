### Three Q&A scenarios

**1. Visitor:** "When is the Choice Market open, and what can I get there?"

**Web concierge:** HomeFront's Choice Market food pantry is at 1880 Princeton Avenue in Lawrenceville. It's open Mondays 10am-2pm, Tuesdays 2pm-6pm, and Thursdays 10am-2pm. You can choose nonperishable food. Fresh produce, dairy, diapers, and items like soap are offered when available. You can return every 30 days.

**Source:** https://www.homefrontnj.org/Get-Help

**Safe next step:** Share the address and hours. If these times don't work, offer The 222 (by appointment) or the other food resources linked on the page.

**2. Visitor:** "My family may have to leave our apartment. Who do I call tonight?"

**Web concierge:** HomeFront's Get Help page says: on weekdays 9am-5pm, call HomeFront at 609-989-9417. On weekdays after 4:30pm, call the Mercer County Board of Social Services (MCBOSS) at 609-468-8296. On weekends, call 2-1-1. If you're experiencing domestic violence, Younity's line is 609-394-9000. If you're in danger, call 911.

**Source:** https://www.homefrontnj.org/Get-Help

**Safe next step:** Show the numbers that match the day and time. The concierge doesn't promise shelter or rental help. The page notes that rental assistance funding is currently very limited.

**3. Visitor:** "Do I need an appointment for The 222 in Trenton?"

**Web concierge:** Yes. HomeFront's page says The 222: Community Market & Resource Hub at 222 N Hermitage Avenue in Trenton is open for food distribution by appointment, Wednesdays and Fridays from 10:30am to 1:30pm. You can book through the appointment link on the Get Help page.

**Source:** https://www.homefrontnj.org/Get-Help

**Safe next step:** Hand off to the booking link on the Get Help page. The concierge never books on the visitor's behalf.

### Privacy, safety and accuracy guardrails

- No eligibility decisions for rental assistance, shelter, Homes by TLC housing or programs; no promises of funds (the page says Housing Stability funding is extremely low).
- Collects no names, addresses, income or case details; reminds visitors not to share them.
- Emergency and DV routing exactly as published: 911, MCBOSS, 2-1-1, Younity, Anchor House.
- Quotes only published hours and appointment rules; when unsure, sends visitors to the front desk.
- No legal or medical advice.
- Disclaimer on screen: independent Divigner demonstration using publicly available information; not an official HomeFront service.
