### Three Q&A scenarios

**1. Visitor:** "How do I apply for help with my heating bill, and what do I need?"

**Web concierge:** Norwescap runs LIHEAP, which helps with heating and cooling bills from October through June. Applications start in the NJ DCAid portal and are approved by the State of New Jersey. You'll need documents for each household member, like proof of income and your utility bills. Norwescap posts a full checklist in English and Spanish. I can't tell you what benefit you'd get. The amount depends on household income, household size, fuel type and region.

**Source:** https://norwescap.org/programs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program/

**Safe next step:** Link the DCAid portal and the document checklist PDF. Offer an application-station appointment or an Engagement Partner booking.

**2. Visitor:** "I applied for energy assistance. How do I check my status?"

**Web concierge:** Norwescap's FAQ says to contact NJ 211 by dialing 211 or 1-877-652-1148 for the most up-to-date status, or to sign in to the NJ DCAid portal. Have your UAP number ready. Norwescap also says to allow at least two weeks for review after a digital submission.

**Source:** https://norwescap.org/contact-us/

**Safe next step:** Route to NJ 211 or DCAid. The concierge doesn't look up applications.

**3. Visitor:** "Can my 4-year-old go to Head Start if we live in Morris County?"

**Web concierge:** Norwescap's Head Start page lists Morris among the counties served, along with Hunterdon, Sussex and Warren. Families generally need to fall within 100% of the federal poverty guidelines or meet one of the listed criteria. Head Start centers offer free full-day programs for ages 3 to 5. Only the program can confirm eligibility, so please call the location nearest you, listed on the page.

**Source:** https://norwescap.org/programs/head-start/

**Safe next step:** Link the page's location contacts or the Engagement Partner booking. Flag internally: the page's locator line omits Morris, so confirm before any pilot.

### Languages (verified only)

English, plus Spanish only where Norwescap publishes Spanish material (LIHEAP document checklist and forms, the Spanish-speaker contact option). The GTranslate widget is machine translation and isn't treated as verified language support.

### Privacy, safety and accuracy guardrails

- No eligibility or benefit determinations for LIHEAP, Head Start, WIC or any program; never quotes benefit amounts.
- Collects no income, SSN, UAP numbers, account numbers or documents; tells visitors not to share them.
- Application status goes only to NJ 211 and DCAid; the concierge never implies it can see an application.
- States that Norwescap doesn't provide immediate emergency housing (per its FAQ); routes emergencies to 911 and 2-1-1.
- County coverage quoted per program page; where pages conflict (Head Start and Morris), it advises calling the program.
- Disclaimer on screen: independent Divigner demonstration using publicly available information; not an official Northwest New Jersey Community Action Program Inc. (Norwescap) service.
