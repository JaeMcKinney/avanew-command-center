**Use cases**
- [Verified] Many entry points (kitchen, shelters, treatment, mental health, peer recovery, job training) share one generic contact form, and public facts conflict across pages (meal counts, breakfast days). A concierge can answer from one reconciled, staff-approved fact set.

### Three Q&A scenarios

**1. Visitor:** "What time is lunch at the Community Kitchen on Saturday? Do I need to sign up?"

**Web concierge:** Eva's website says one lunch is served on Saturdays and Sundays at 12:00 PM, and that all are welcome. The page doesn't mention signing up. On weekdays, breakfast is at 8:30 am and lunch is at 12:00 pm and 1:00 pm. Schedules can change, so for anything special you can call Eva's main line at 973-523-6220.

**Source:** https://www.evasvillage.org/communitykitchen.html

**Safe next step:** Offer the main line 973-523-6220 and the kitchen page link. No sign-up rules are stated, so the concierge doesn't invent any.

**2. Visitor:** "¿Tienen terapeutas que hablen español para mi hija? ¿Cómo pido una cita?"

**Web concierge:** Sí. La página de salud mental de Eva's Village dice que tienen terapeutas que hablan español. El programa ambulatorio de salud mental está licenciado por el Departamento de Servicios Humanos de NJ, y la persona primero se reúne con un terapeuta para una evaluación. Para pedir información o una cita, puede llamar al 973-523-6220 o usar el formulario de "Get Help". No puedo dar consejos clínicos. Si es una emergencia, llame al 911 o al 988.

**Source:** https://www.evasvillage.org/mental-health.html

**Safe next step:** Main line 973-523-6220 or the Get Help form (https://www.evasvillage.org/ineedhelp.html). Remind the visitor not to type health details into the demo.

**3. Visitor:** "My brother wants to stop drinking. Is there somewhere he can talk to someone who's been through it?"

**Web concierge:** Eva's Recovery Community Center in downtown Paterson is a volunteer, peer-run center that offers free recovery coaching, peer support groups, and referrals. It isn't a treatment program. It's open Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm. The after-hours help line is (973) 381-0425, and the page asks people to call ahead between 4:00 and 9:00 pm. If your brother is in danger or having a medical emergency, call 911. For a mental health or substance use crisis, call or text 988.

**Source:** https://www.evasvillage.org/rcc.html

**Safe next step:** Give the RCC number and hours as published. Offer the outpatient program page for treatment information. No clinical advice and no questions about his use.

### Languages (verified only)

English and Spanish only. Spanish is verified from Eva's Spanish-language page and the Spanish-speaking therapists noted on the mental health page. No other languages are offered in the demo.

### Privacy, safety and accuracy guardrails

- No clinical, medical, legal or treatment advice; no admission or eligibility decisions for halfway houses, shelters or outpatient care.
- Never asks for or stores names, health, substance-use or insurance details (42 CFR Part 2 and HIPAA context). A visible notice asks visitors not to share them.
- Crisis routing first: 911 for emergencies, 988 for mental health or substance use crises, then the RCC line.
- Uses only published hours. Where Eva's pages conflict (meal counts, breakfast days, shelter numbers), the concierge says "please confirm with staff" and gives the main line.
- Respectful, non-judgmental recovery language ("person in recovery", not labels).
- Disclaimer on screen: independent Divigner demonstration using publicly available information; not an official Eva's Village service.
