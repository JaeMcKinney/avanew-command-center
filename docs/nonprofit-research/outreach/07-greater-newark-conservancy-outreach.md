**Featured programs (2 to 3, official links)**

1. **Food Initiatives: farm stands, farmers markets and CSA produce boxes** (https://greaternewark.org/urban-agriculture/). Seasonal mobile farm stands and markets accept SNAP/EBT and WIC; Double Bucks (introduced 2024) doubles the value of SNAP dollars for fruits and vegetables. Biweekly CSA boxes picked up at 32 Prince Street, sold through https://greaternewark.store/ (Fall 2026 program: order deadline Sept 1, 2026; pickups Thursdays 3 to 7 PM and Fridays 3 to 5 PM). Market dates are on https://greaternewark.org/our-events/.
2. **Plot-It-Fresh Community Garden Program** (https://greaternewark.org/plot-it-fresh-community-garden-program/). Garden beds, tools, seeds and guidance at partner gardens in the West, South and North Wards (for example 144 Smith Street). The 2026 season ran May to November; 2026 applications were accepted on a rolling basis in February, prioritizing Newark residents, one plot per household. Questions: plotitfresh@greaternewark.org or (973) 642-4646.
3. **Family Programs and the Prudential Outdoor Learning Center** (https://greaternewark.org/programs/ and https://greaternewark.org/visit-our-garden/). Second Saturday Family Adventures and Family Fun in the Kitchen cooking classes; garden open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM; group tours by email to the education contact listed on the page.
