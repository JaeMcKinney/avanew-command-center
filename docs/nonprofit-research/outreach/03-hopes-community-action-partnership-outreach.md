## D. Demo package

**Personalized greeting (spoken and on screen)**
"Hi, welcome. I'm a web concierge built from HOPES Community Action Partnership's public website, as an independent Divigner demonstration. I can show you HOPES' early childhood programs, free tax preparation, and other family services, and point you to the right next step. Please don't share names, birthdates or income. **Q&A scenario 1: Early Head Start**
- Visitor: "My son is almost two. Does HOPES have a program for him, and how do I apply?"
- Answer: "HOPES' Infants and Toddlers program, which is Early Head Start, serves infants and toddlers with health and developmental screenings, parent education, and classroom or home-based options. Entry depends on available space and income eligibility. You can start with the online application, and HOPES staff will follow up with you about eligibility and next steps."
- Source: https://www.hopes.org/infants-and-toddlers
- Safe next step: show the "Apply Today" link on that page and 1-855-OK-HOPES; state that HOPES staff decide eligibility.

**Q&A scenario 2: Head Start preschool**
- Visitor: "What is the preschool day like at HOPES?"
- Answer: "HOPES Preschool includes Head Start and state-funded classes. The program page lists a 6-hour class day, a 180-day program, and maximum class sizes of 15 in Hoboken and Plainfield and 17 in Somerset. Children receive developmental and health screenings, and families get parent workshops and father-involvement activities. The Hoboken program has been NAEYC-accredited since 2007."
- Source: https://www.hopes.org/preschool-head-start
- Safe next step: link the application and the "Contact Us About Preschool Program" form.

**Q&A scenario 3: free tax help**
- Visitor: "Can HOPES do my taxes for free? Can I just walk in?"
- Answer: "HOPES offers free federal and state tax preparation (New Jersey and New York) by IRS-certified staff and volunteers. All tax services are by appointment only, through HOPES' automated scheduler or by calling 201-468-8805. HOPES asks you to bring proof of income for your most recent 4 weeks of work. HOPES can't give refund estimates."
- Source: https://www.hopes.org/volunteer-income-tax-assistance
- Safe next step: link https://hopes.cascheduler.com; no tax advice.
