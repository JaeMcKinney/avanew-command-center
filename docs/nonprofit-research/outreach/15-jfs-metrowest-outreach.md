### Three Q&A scenarios

**1. Visitor:** "Does JFS take my insurance for counseling? What if I don't have insurance?"

**Web concierge:** JFS's counseling page says it accepts most major insurances, including Medicare and Medicaid. Private pay and sliding-scale options are available for people without health insurance. For details about your plan, call (973) 765-9050. Please don't share your insurance information with me.

**Source:** https://www.jfsmetrowest.org/counseling/

**Safe next step:** Call (973) 765-9050 or use the contact form with 'Counseling/Mental Health' selected. No clinical advice.

**2. Visitor:** "I'm caring for my mother, who has dementia. What help is there for caregivers?"

**Web concierge:** JFS offers Care Consultation, a free, telephone-based program for family caregivers and the people they care for. Its social workers are licensed clinicians. JFS also has a free Caregiver Respite Program, where a screened, trained volunteer visits your loved one so you can take a break. To learn more, call (973) 637-1740 or email caregivers@jfsmetrowest.org.

**Source:** https://www.jfsmetrowest.org/older-adults/

**Safe next step:** Share the caregiver line and email. The concierge doesn't assess the mother's condition.

**3. Visitor:** "How do I get help from the Rachel Coalition?"

**Web concierge:** The Rachel Coalition is JFS's domestic violence program. It offers counseling, legal services and court advocacy. Call (973) 740-1233. If you're in immediate danger, call 911. The JFS page has a Quick Exit button if you need to leave the site quickly.

**Source:** https://www.jfsmetrowest.org/domestic-violence-rachel-coalition/

**Safe next step:** Give the Rachel number and 911, and point out the Quick Exit. The concierge asks no questions about the situation.

### Privacy, safety and accuracy guardrails

- No clinical, medical or legal advice; no diagnosis or medication talk; no therapist matching.
- Collects no health, insurance, DV or personal details; reminds visitors not to share them (HIPAA context).
- States that JFS is not a crisis center; routes emergencies to 911, 988 and the JFS Emergency Resources page.
- DV visitors see the Quick Exit reminder and the Rachel Coalition line; no questions about their situation.
- No eligibility decisions for DDD support coordination, survivor homecare or financial aid.
- Respectful of JFS's Jewish communal context and Federation partnership; no claims of affiliation.
- Disclaimer on screen: independent Divigner demonstration using publicly available information; not an official Jewish Family Service of MetroWest NJ service.
