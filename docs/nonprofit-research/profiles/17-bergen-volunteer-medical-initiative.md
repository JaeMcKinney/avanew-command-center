- **Patient eligibility (published):** adults 18+ living in Bergen County; someone in the household must be working; income up to 250% of the Federal Poverty Level; no health insurance. Applicants are screened for financial and medical eligibility and must provide proof of Bergen County residency, proof of earned income, and a complete medical history. Screening repeats every year (https://www.bvmi.org/services) [V].
- **Languages:** The website has Spanish (https://www.bvmi.org/es/) and Korean (https://www.bvmi.org/ko/) versions, apparently machine-translated [V/I]. The clinic uses "language interpreters" (https://www.bvmi.org/our-organization) [V]. Which languages staff speak is not published [U].
- **Hours:**
  - Hackensack: Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri 9:30 AM–4 PM; Tue 11 AM–7 PM.
  - Garfield: Wed/Thu 9:30 AM–4 PM only.
  - Telehealth: an NP is available for phone consults and sick visits (https://www.bvmi.org/our-locations ; https://www.bvmi.org/our-organization) [V].
- **Intake/enrollment path:** Call 201-342-2478 (Hackensack) or 551-380-4800 (Garfield); "Email Us" on the contact page; then eligibility screening with documents [V]. No online pre-screening tool was found [I].
- **Existing chat/FAQ/after-hours info:** No chat widget detected in the homepage HTML [V]. No patient FAQ page found [I]. After hours, the only routes are the phone lines and the static Services page [I].
- **Note:** BVMI's Garfield clinic (529 Midland Ave) shares an address with CFBNJ's Garfield Unity Pantry (prospect #10) [V].

## Fit assessment

- **Evidence for fit:** A detailed published eligibility test (residency, working household, 250% FPL, uninsured, documents) that prospective patients must work through. Two sites with very different hours, one of them new. Spanish and Korean web versions plus interpreters signal multilingual demand. The patient population works during the day; Hackensack's only evening hours are Tuesday until 7 PM [V/I].
- **Risks:** Health-care context. The demo must avoid any medical advice or eligibility determination and be careful around HIPAA-adjacent data [I]. The budget is still modest and campaign-driven [I]. About 1,000 active patients is a relatively small population, though prospective-patient inquiries are likely larger [U].
- **Unknowns:** Volume of eligibility calls; waitlist status; how the Korean and Spanish pages are maintained [U].
- **Local meeting practicality:** Good. Hackensack is about 25 min away and Garfield about 20 min [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)

- **Value angle:** Someone in Bergen County who is working but uninsured has to answer four questions before calling: Do I live in the county? Is someone in my household working? Am I under 250% of poverty? Am I uninsured? Then they need to know which documents to bring and whether Hackensack or Garfield is open. A concierge built on BVMI's public pages could explain all of this at any hour, in English, Spanish or Korean, without ever deciding eligibility, and route people to the right clinic line.

## Live demo plan (independent prototype)

- **Three tailored avatar questions:**
  1. "I work full time but have no insurance. Can I be a patient at BVMI?" (Reads the four published criteria and the documents list, says that BVMI makes the decision through its screening, and gives 201-342-2478.)
  2. "¿Qué documentos necesito llevar para inscribirme?" (Proof of Bergen County residency, proof of earned income, complete medical history; screening repeats annually.)
  3. "Is the Garfield clinic open on Tuesday?" (No. Garfield is open Wed/Thu 9:30 AM–4 PM; Hackensack is open Tuesday 11 AM–7 PM; gives both numbers.)
- **Knowledge-base source URLs (public pages only):** https://www.bvmi.org/services · https://www.bvmi.org/our-locations · https://www.bvmi.org/contact-us · https://www.bvmi.org/primary-care · https://www.bvmi.org/diabetes-care-and-education · https://www.bvmi.org/womens-health-initiative · https://www.bvmi.org/chronic-disease-management · https://www.bvmi.org/mental-health · https://www.bvmi.org/our-organization · https://www.bvmi.org/volunteer-medical-general · https://www.bvmi.org/es/ · https://www.bvmi.org/ko/
