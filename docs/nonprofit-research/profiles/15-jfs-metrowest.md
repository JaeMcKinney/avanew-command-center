## Fit assessment
- **Evidence for fit:** Eight service lines with distinct eligibility (insurance and Medicaid, DDD eligibility, survivor status, sliding scale). Two offices. A 7-county DDD footprint. Common caregiver and older-adult questions. A clear, well-structured site. Program revenue is growing.

The staff page was last updated December 2024 [V]. Outreach must be sensitive to the agency's Jewish communal context and Federation relationships [I].
