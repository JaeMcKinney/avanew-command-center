## Fit assessment
- **Evidence for fit:** Very large public intake (30K+ individuals). Pantry schedules that vary by site and day. Multiple campuses. A housing crisis line. Rich, well-structured web content to build a knowledge base from. A dedicated strategy and innovation officer.
- **Risks:** About 55 miles from Montclair, so in-person meetings are impractical for routine work [I]. HomeFront already publishes good after-hours routing, so the "gap" is smaller [V/I]. There are two recent operating deficits [V]. The organization defines itself as Central NJ, outside Divigner's target geography [V].
- **Unknowns:** Inquiry volume; languages requested; how the hotline is staffed; internal tech plans.
- **Local meeting practicality:** Low. A day trip of about 65–75 minutes each way [I]. Prefer a video first meeting.
- **Tier caveat:** The rubric total (22) lands exactly on the A threshold even with a 0 for proximity. I recommend treating HomeFront as a secondary or later-wave A, behind the North Jersey A-tier prospects, consistent with the spec's North Jersey focus [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)
- **Value angle:** HomeFront already tells families exactly whom to call after 4:30pm and on weekends. A concierge could carry that same clarity into every question: Choice Market and The 222 hours, Hire Expectations credentials, FreeStore and diaper access. It would answer in the moment, in the visitor's language, from HomeFront's own pages, so the front desk spends less time on routine logistics.
- **Day-0 founder email**
  - Subject: Carrying HomeFront's "Get Help" clarity to every hour
  - Body:

    Hi Sarah,

    I'm Jae McKinney, founder of Divigner. I'm currently working with New Community Corporation on a community engagement initiative, and I've been studying how organizations help families find the right door quickly. Your Get Help page is one of the clearest I've seen: after-hours routing, Choice Market and The 222 schedules, and FreeStore details.

    I built a short interactive example that answers those questions conversationally from your public pages and hands people to your team: [demo link]. It is an independent Divigner prototype, not affiliated with or endorsed by HomeFront, and it uses only public information.

    Would you or Michael Nordquist be open to a brief video call to tell me whether this could save your front desk time?

    Best,
    Jae McKinney
    Divigner | jae@divigner.com
- **LinkedIn note:** Hi Sarah, I'm Jae, founder of Divigner. I build tools that help families find nonprofit services after hours. I emailed you a short, independent example drawn from HomeFront's public Get Help pages and would be glad to connect.
