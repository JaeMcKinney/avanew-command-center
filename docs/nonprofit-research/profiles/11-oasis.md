- **Eligibility [V]:** Serves women and children at or below the federal poverty line. "All Oasis program participants are eligible for NJ SNAP (the measure used to validate need)." (https://oasisnj.org/what-we-do/)
- **Languages:** Website is English only; no Spanish pages or translation widget were found [V]. Domestic violence counseling includes "weekly group sessions conducted in English and Spanish" [V] (social-services page). A large ESL program (501 ESL participants in 2025) points to substantial Spanish-speaking and other non-English-speaking demand [I].
- **Service area:** Greater Paterson [V] (who-we-are page).
- **Scale (site, 2024–2025):** about 1,500 women and children served daily; 100,642 meals; 893 adult students in 2025 [V].
- **Hours:** Meals run weekdays; exact meal times are not published [U]. Donation drop-off is 8–11:30am and 1–2:30pm Mon–Fri, per an Oasis Instagram post shown on the homepage, October 2026 [V]. Thrift store hours were not captured [U].
- **Intake path:** Walk-in. Women entering the dining room are introduced to services by social workers, and an initial psychosocial assessment follows [V]. The site has no online enrollment form [I]. Main phone is (973) 881-8307 [V].
- **Existing chat/FAQ/after-hours:** No chatbot, FAQ page or after-hours guidance was detected (scraped homepage and program pages, 2026-10-28) [I]. The site's /contact/ URL returns a 404; contact information lives in the footer [V].

## Fit assessment
- **Evidence for fit:** High-volume walk-in services (about 1,500 people a day). More than 14 distinct programs, each with its own eligibility and schedule. Strong multilingual demand (ESL, Spanish-language groups). An English-only site with no FAQ and no published meal or enrollment times. Well-documented staff directory. Paterson is a short drive from Montclair. [V/I]
- **Risks:** Serves domestic violence survivors, so the demo must avoid collecting personal data and must route crises correctly. Funding is mostly private donors, so budget for a new tool may be tight [I]. The FY2025 surplus narrowed to $0.1M. The highest-value languages (Spanish and others) would need the multilingual add-on [I].
- **Unknowns:** Actual call or inquiry volume. Whether staff are fielding repeat questions. Thrift store and meal hours. Current tech stack.
- **Local meeting practicality:** Very practical; 59 Mill St is about 20–25 minutes from Montclair [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)
- **Value angle:** Many families first meet Oasis through the weekday soup kitchen and then need to know about ESL levels, GED prep, the Citizenship course, childcare during class, or the After-School Academy. An always-on concierge could answer those first questions at any hour from Oasis's own public pages, then hand people to the right staff member. Social workers could spend their time on assessments and crisis support.
- **Day-0 founder email**
  - Subject: An after-hours guide to Oasis programs for Paterson families
  - Body:

    Hi Jennifer,

    I'm Jae McKinney, founder of Divigner in Montclair. I'm currently working with New Community Corporation on a community engagement initiative, and Oasis came to mind because so many women find you through the weekday meals and then need to learn about ESL, GED prep, the Citizenship course, or the After-School Academy.

    I built a short interactive example from your public website. It answers those first questions at any hour and points people to the right Oasis staff: [demo link]. It is an independent Divigner prototype, not affiliated with or endorsed by Oasis, and it uses only public information.

    Would you be open to a 20-minute call, or a short visit at Mill Street, to tell me whether something like this would help your team?

    Best,
    Jae McKinney
    Divigner | jae@divigner.com
