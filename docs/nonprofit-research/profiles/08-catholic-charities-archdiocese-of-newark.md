## Mission and programs

**Mission** (https://ccannj.org/about-us/): "In response to the Gospel of Jesus Christ, Catholic Charities, as a ministry of the Archdiocese of Newark, serves the poor and marginalized and advocates for their needs in order to promote the inherent dignity and worth of all people without regard to race, religion, or culture." [V]

Scale (homepage impact panel): "55,160 individuals served in 86 programs at 26 sites." That includes 201,560 shelter nights for 3,264 people, 196,905 mental-health services for 3,451 people, legal services for 6,052 immigrants, 4,994 refugees, food for 30,636 households, and $8.8M in financial assistance [V].

## Fit assessment

- **Evidence for fit:** It has 86 programs at 26 sites in four counties, more than 55,000 people served, and one help line plus dozens of program numbers. The population is high-need and multilingual (immigrants, refugees, Hispanic older adults, Haitian community). The website is English-only. The value case is "help me find the right door" [V/I].
- **Risks:**
  - The 2023 data breach makes privacy the first question [V/I].
  - Structurally negative net assets and a thin margin may limit discretionary spending [V/I].
  - As an archdiocesan ministry, it likely has layered approvals [I].
  - A website with a bot challenge complicates building a knowledge base [V].
- **Unknowns:** CEO compensation, why there is no Schedule R, a communications or IT lead, and which languages front-line staff speak [U].
- **Local meeting practicality:** Newark HQ is about 15–20 minutes from Montclair. This is the closest of this batch [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)

- **Value angle:** Someone searching at night for shelter, utility help, or an immigration appointment faces 86 programs and many phone numbers. A concierge built from CCAN's public pages and catalog could steer them to the right program, hours and number, starting with the 1-800 Help Line. It would collect no personal information, which matters after 2023. A pilot could start with one division, such as Housing & Food.
