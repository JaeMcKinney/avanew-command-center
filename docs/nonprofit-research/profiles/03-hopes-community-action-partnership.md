## Fit assessment

- **Evidence for fit:** There are 11 centers in three counties with one toll-free number. About ten public programs each have their own eligibility rules and document requirements ("bring 4 weeks of proof of income"). Families apply online and wait for follow-up. The site is bilingual. Services run strictly 9–5 on weekdays. This is a textbook case for after-hours, bilingual program navigation [V/I].
- **Risks:** Heavy federal dependence (Head Start) could make leadership cautious about new spending [I]. Head Start privacy and enrollment rules mean the demo must never collect child or family data [I]. A Wix Chat inbox may already exist [I].
- **Unknowns:** call or inquiry volumes, current front-desk staffing, whether Wix Chat is active, and the cause of the liability growth [U].
- **Local meeting practicality:** The Hoboken HQ is about 30 minutes from Montclair, which makes an in-person meeting easy. Plainfield and Somerset are farther [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)

- **Value angle:** HOPES already routes everything through 1-855-OK-HOPES and a 9-to-5 front door. A bilingual concierge could answer the evening and weekend questions families actually ask: how to apply for Early Head Start, what to bring for benefits counseling, how to book a VITA slot, whether a senior qualifies for a ride. Then it points them to the right center or form. Staff time goes to follow-up, not repeated explanations.
