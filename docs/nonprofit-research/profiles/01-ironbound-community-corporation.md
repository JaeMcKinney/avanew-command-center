## Fit assessment

**Evidence for fit**
- About 12 distinct programs across 5 sites, with zip-code, income, and age eligibility rules [V].
- A heavily immigrant, multilingual service area [V, ICC's data].
- Paper-based, day-of-week application windows (repeat "when and how do I apply" questions are likely) [I].
- High benefits-enrollment volume in ICC's own 2024 report: for example, 1,624 NJ FamilyCare and 440 LIHEAP assists at the Family Success Centers [V, read from a designed PDF, so confirm before quoting] (S6).
- English-only website [V].

**Risks**
- **ICC published an op-ed on 2026-09-29, "Newark does not need AI data centers,"** calling for a municipal moratorium (S15). An "AI avatar" pitch could read as off-mission. Lead strictly with resident access and language access, and be ready to answer environmental-footprint and data-handling questions candidly.
- Strong organizing and justice identity: expect scrutiny of transcript retention and immigrant-privacy safeguards, given that ICC is active on ICE accountability (S5).
- CFO listing conflict between the site and the 990.

**Unknowns:** Inquiry volumes, staff count, current CFO, and appetite for vendor technology.

**Local meeting practicality:** Easy. 317 Elm St is about 25 min from Montclair [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)

- **Value angle:** Ironbound families juggle different application windows, zip-code rules, and three languages across five sites. A concierge that answers "which program, where, when, and what do I bring?" in Spanish and Portuguese, outside office hours, could cut repeat calls to the Family Success Centers and the ELC. It should always route people to ICC staff for anything personal.
