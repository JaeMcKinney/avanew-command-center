## Fit assessment

- **Evidence for fit:** Walk-in meals, shelters, residential and outpatient treatment, mental health and peer recovery all run from one campus. There are many entry points and a single generic contact form. A Spanish-speaking population is served. Partial after-hours coverage exists (RCC to 9 p.m., OORP 24h). Public facts on the site conflict with each other. A concierge could give consistent answers ("When is lunch? How do I get into a halfway house? Who do I call tonight?") [V/I].
- **Risks:**
  - Two years of deficits make discretionary spend harder [V].
  - SUD and mental-health questions carry clinical and crisis risk, so strict escalation and 988/911 routing are required [I].
  - 42 CFR Part 2 and HIPAA sensitivities mean no personal data at all [I].
- **Unknowns:** office hours, admissions process for halfway houses, inquiry volume, and whether communications staff maintain the site [U].
- **Local meeting practicality:** Paterson is about 20–25 minutes from Montclair, so an on-site visit is easy [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)

- **Value angle:** Eva's front door covers a soup kitchen, two shelters, halfway houses, outpatient treatment, mental health and peer recovery. Today it's a single contact form and a 4–9 p.m. help line. A bilingual concierge could give the same accurate answer every time (meal times, how admissions start, who to call tonight) and route urgent needs to the RCC line, OORP or 988, without staff repeating themselves.
