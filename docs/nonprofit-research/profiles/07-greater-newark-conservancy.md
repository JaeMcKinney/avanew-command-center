- **Value angle:** Newark families want quick answers: when the next farmers market is, how to get a produce box, whether the garden is open, how to book a field trip. A friendly multilingual concierge could answer these from GNC's own pages around the clock and steer people to events, volunteering, and the CHFNS coalition, freeing a lean team for programs.
