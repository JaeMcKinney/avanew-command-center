## Fit assessment

- **Evidence for fit:** Several public audiences at once: would-be homeowners checking eligibility and waitlists, homeowners asking about repairs (closed to new applications), residents using the Hub pantry, ID restoration and computer lab, ReStore shoppers and donors across two stores, and volunteers. There is a Spanish-language contact. The public must navigate two websites and five named staff contacts. It is in Newark, about 20 minutes from Montclair, which fits the NCC/Newark narrative closely [V/I].
- **Risks:** **Financial strain.** Three widening deficits and net assets down to $548K make new spending hard to approve [V/I]. A small staff (28) spread across construction, retail and community services [V].
- **Unknowns:** Inquiry volume; ReStore hours and donation-pickup policies; whether HFHI supplies affiliate web or chat tools [U].
- **Local meeting practicality:** Excellent. Newark is about 20 min away and Fairfield about 15 min [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)

- **Value angle:** Habitat Greater Newark serves would-be homeowners, repair applicants, Hub neighbors and ReStore donors, all through a lean team and named staff inboxes. A concierge built on Habitat's public pages could answer the repeat questions at any hour: am I eligible for homeownership, is the repair program open, when is the Wednesday pantry, how do I donate to the ReStore. It could do this in English or Spanish, so staff time goes to families and builds.
