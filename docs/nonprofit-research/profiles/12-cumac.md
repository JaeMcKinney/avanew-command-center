## Mission and programs

**Mission** (homepage and 990 Part I): "CUMAC's mission is to fight hunger and its root causes through a holistic, trauma-informed approach that provides groceries and basic necessities to families and individuals in need." [V]

| Program | What it is | Who it serves / eligibility | Location | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Choice Marketplace | Appointment-based, client-choice grocery market for more than 72,000 people a year. Up to one appointment per month; slots 9:30–2:30 M–F plus Wednesday late shopping 4–6 pm | Passaic County residents only. New-guest registration 10 am–12 pm M–F with photo ID for each household member and proof of Passaic County address. Proxy shoppers allowed | 223 Ellison St, Paterson | https://www.cumac.org/help [V] |
| Mobile Pantry | Mobile food and application support in all 16 Passaic County municipalities; online/QR registration | Household income at or below 185% FPL, or categorical eligibility (TANF, SNAP, SSI, WIC, Medicaid, disaster). Can't combine with the marketplace in the same month | County-wide | https://www.cumac.org/mobile-pantry [V] |
| Benefits Application Support / SNAP Navigators | Certified SNAP navigators; help with SNAP, Medicaid, LIHEAP; "full-loop navigation." Detailed document checklist | Passaic County residents; 9:30–4:00 M–F | Paterson | https://www.cumac.org/help [V] |
| Home delivery | Grocery home delivery (990 Part III) | Unknown | Passaic County | 990 TY2024 [V] |

- **Languages:** The help page says staff speak "Spanish, Bangladeshi [sic], Arabic, and Filipino." SNAP work-requirement guidance is posted in English and Spanish, and the site has a Google Translate widget [V].
- **Service area:** Passaic County only. Non-residents are referred elsewhere [V].
- **Hours:** Marketplace M–F 9:30–3:00 (footer). Appointments 9:30–2:30, Wednesday to 6 pm. Closed the first Friday of every month ("Wellness Fridays") and every Friday in July [V].
- **Intake path:** Walk-in registration windows; appointments; rescheduling by email (support@cumacecho.org) or text to 973-742-5518 "at the end of the work day"; a SNAP navigator request form; mobile-pantry online registration [V].
- **Existing chat/FAQ/after-hours:** It has a strong public FAQ (Get Help page) but no chatbot. The "chat with us" text is an event blurb. Rescheduling requests arrive by email or text after hours [V].

## Fit assessment

- **Evidence for fit:** This is direct public intake with precise rules: county residency, document lists, one appointment a month, proxies, a marketplace and mobile pantry that can't be combined, ABAWD work requirements, and changing SNAP rules after the November 2025 benefit disruption. Staff speak at least four non-English languages. Reschedules arrive after hours by text and email. The existing FAQ is already a ready-made knowledge base [V/I].
- **Risks:** a small team and thin margins; volunteer-heavy operations; benefit rules change often, so the knowledge base must be maintained; there's a risk of residents treating answers as eligibility decisions [I].
- **Unknowns:** inquiry and reschedule volumes, existing phone or IVR setup, home-delivery eligibility, and housing program details [U].
- **Local meeting practicality:** Paterson is about 20–25 minutes from Montclair, and CUMAC already offers tours [V/I].
