One deficit year is not yet a trend, so no adjustment was made [I]. **The organization depends almost entirely on government funding:** line 1e government grants were $24,439,515, 92.9% of revenue (Head Start, WIC, LIHEAP and similar) [V/I]. That creates exposure to federal funding cycles [I].

## Fit assessment
- **Evidence for fit:** 26 programs, each with its own eligibility, county coverage and documentation rules. Seasonal surges in high-volume questions (LIHEAP runs October–June; the FAQ already fields "where is my application?"). A 5–6 county footprint. A Spanish-speaker request path. An existing Engagement Partner model that already frames navigation as a service.
- **Risks:** Distance: about 65–75 minutes from Montclair [I]. With 93% of revenue from government grants, procurement may be formal and budget-constrained [I]. Energy-assistance status questions belong to NJ 211 and DCAid, and the avatar must not imply otherwise [V]. FY2025 was a deficit year [V].
- **Unknowns:** Inquiry volumes by season; the languages actually requested; whether the Engagement Partner team is at capacity; Morris County site addresses (Head Start serves Morris).
- **Local meeting practicality:** Low to moderate. A dedicated half-day trip to Phillipsburg. A Morris County Head Start site may be closer; its address was not verified.
