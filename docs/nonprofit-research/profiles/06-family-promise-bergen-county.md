## Fit assessment

- **Evidence for fit:** Two different public audiences: families facing eviction or homelessness, and anyone who needs a meal. There is a published eligibility list, a Spanish help page, phone-only intake ("we do not accept walk-ins"), and a time-sensitive status message (rental assistance closed). A nightly dinner of 160–170 meals is served after typical office hours [V].
- **Risks:** Smallest budget among my five prospects ($1.94M), so even a modest monthly fee is a real line item. Thin staff (12). The office is moving to Hackensack soon, which could absorb leadership attention. Funding is almost entirely private, with two small deficits [V/I].
- **Unknowns:** Call volume; office hours; whether the national Family Promise network supplies any digital tools; a general contact email [U].
- **Local meeting practicality:** Easy. Ridgewood and Hackensack are each about 25–30 minutes from Montclair [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)

- **Value angle:** Families searching late at night need two answers fast: "Can I get a meal tonight?" and "Can Family Promise help me keep my apartment?" A concierge built on Family Promise's own pages could give the dinner hours and location, explain the published housing-program criteria, give the current status of rental assistance (in English or Spanish), and point people to the 201-833-8009 intake line. It would never decide anyone's eligibility.

## Live demo plan (independent prototype)

- **Three tailored avatar questions:**
  1. "Is there somewhere in Hackensack I can get a free dinner tonight, and what time?" (Walk-In Dinner, 120 South River St, daily 4:45–5:50 PM.)
  2. "I'm working full time but I'm behind on rent. Can Family Promise help me?" (It explains that rental-assistance applications are currently closed, gives the published program criteria, and points to the 201-833-8009 intake line. No eligibility determination.)
  3. "¿Tienen ayuda para familias sin hogar con niños?" (Spanish answer drawn from the Spanish help page plus the Bridge program description.)

## Corrections to preliminary compilation

- The preliminary marked EIN, revenue and every 990 field "not yet verified." They are now verified: EIN 22-2853599 and TY2024 revenue of $1,939,402, which passes the $1.5M screen by about $440K.
