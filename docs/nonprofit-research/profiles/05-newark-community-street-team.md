## Fit assessment

**Evidence for fit**
- About 9 distinct programs with age and county eligibility rules [V].
- Its FAQ shows a steady flow of repeat public questions [V].
- Several intake routes, including hotlines and web forms [V].
- Leadership with national CVI visibility (New York Times coverage) [V] (S9).

**Risks (material)**
- NCST's published **research and data policy** ("nothing about us without us"; IRB approval required; community access to all data) and its **refusal to share information with law enforcement** (S4) mean an unsolicited avatar that records transcripts could be read as data extraction from a community it protects. **Any demo must collect no transcripts, or explicitly offer that, and never be pitched as surveillance-adjacent.**
- The clients are crime victims and high-risk youth: safety-critical, with no room for error.
- Funding is under pressure (lost VOCA grant, 30% revenue drop).
- Leadership churn.
- Some contact channels are personal Gmail, a sign of a low-tooling environment.

**Unknowns:** Current staff size, FY2025 finances, current COO and CFO.

**Local meeting practicality:** Good (about 20–25 min) [I].

## Outreach package (draft — do not send)

- **Value angle:** Residents and partners repeatedly ask NCST the same things: how to refer a young person, how to reach the Trauma Recovery Center, whether NCST can staff Safe Passage. A concierge built only from NCST's public pages, designed to **collect no personal data**, could answer those around the clock and route urgent situations straight to NCST's hotline or 911, so outreach workers stay on the street. NCST would decide what's in it.

**Day-0 founder email**

Subject: Answering NCST's most common public questions, on your terms

> Dear Rey,
>
> I'm Jae McKinney, founder of Divigner in Montclair. I'm currently working with New Community Corporation on a community engagement initiative, and NCST's "public safety in public hands" model is one I respect.
>
> Your FAQ shows how often people ask the same questions: how to refer someone aged 14–30, how to reach the Trauma Recovery Center, what Safe Passage involves. I put together a short example of a guide that answers only from your public website and sends anything urgent to your hotline or 911: [demo link]. It's an independent Divigner prototype. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NCST, and I'm glad to switch off any transcript recording.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation, at your office or by phone, about whether this fits your values?
>
> Respectfully,
> Jae McKinney
> Divigner · jae@divigner.com
