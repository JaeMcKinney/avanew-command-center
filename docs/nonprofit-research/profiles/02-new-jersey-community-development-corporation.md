## Fit assessment

**Evidence for fit**
- More than 12 programs; 200+ housing units across 8 properties; 4 community schools; a county NJ4S hub [V].
- Documented high public demand (the housing application backlog) [V].
- A help center that fields housing, education, and immigration questions [V].
- An English-only site in a heavily Spanish-speaking city. Paterson's demographics weren't verified here, so treat that as [I].
- 150 staff [V].

**Risks**
- A large, well-resourced agency may prefer in-house or HubSpot tooling.
- School- and referral-based programs generate less public walk-in inquiry.
- Thin program pages (few hours or eligibility details) limit the knowledge base.
- Leadership page vs. 990 discrepancy on the real-estate chief.

**Unknowns:** Inquiry volumes, languages served, whether HubSpot chat is in use.

**Local meeting practicality:** Good. About 20–25 min to Spruce St, with visitor parking at the Great Falls Welcome Center (S10).
