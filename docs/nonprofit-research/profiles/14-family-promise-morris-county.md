## Mission and programs
**Mission (990 Part I, CY2025):** "To mobilize community resources and people to end the crisis of homelessness faced by Morris County families and individuals by providing a continuum of service." The site's tagline reads: "Transforming the lives of families and individuals by serving our community through all stages of life to prevent or end homelessness and housing insecurity." (https://www.familypromisemorris.org/) [V]

| Program | What it is | Who it serves / eligibility | Location | Source URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shelter Program | Since 1988. Originally congregation-hosted (80+ congregations); since March 2020 a motel-sheltering model with case management and housing specialists | Families and individuals experiencing homelessness | Partner motels; central office | https://www.familypromisemorris.org/shelter-programs |
| Community Support Program | Crisis intervention, in-home case management, referrals | Shelter graduates and precariously housed residents | Community | https://www.familypromisemorris.org/housing-programs |
| Keys to Housing | Up to 40 housing vouchers from local, regional and federal partners; landlord incentives and tenant education | Voucher-eligible households | Morris County | https://www.familypromisemorris.org/housing-programs |
| Our Promise (OP) Drop-In Center | Phone and computer access, mailing address, restrooms, warming/cooling center, information and referral, medical, mental health and dental linkage, case management | Chronically and transiently homeless people, veterans, people leaving jail or hospital, youth, LGBTQ+, precariously housed | First Baptist Church, 51 Washington St, Morristown | https://www.familypromisemorris.org/outreach-programs |
| Coordinated Entry (CE) | One of two county lead agencies; OP is one of two CE access points | People awaiting shelter in Morris County | 51 Washington St | https://www.familypromisemorris.org/outreach-programs |
| Navigating Hope (NH) | Mobile outreach van, with Morris County Human Services, covering all 39 municipalities at least 5 days a week | County residents needing housing, economic or food help | Mobile | https://www.familypromisemorris.org/outreach-programs |

## Corrections to preliminary compilation
- Preliminary said EIN and financials were not verified. Verified: EIN 52-1572014; latest 990 is CY2025 with revenue of $2.88M. It passes the $1.5M screen but sits in the lower band, and revenue declined 14% from 2024.
- The program description ("family homelessness prevention and stabilization") understated the scope. FPMC also serves individuals (the Our Promise drop-in, Coordinated Entry, the Navigating Hope van), not only families.
- The preliminary implied a congregation-hosted shelter model. FPMC has used motel sheltering since March 2020, with congregations as volunteer and meal partners.
- Leadership was missing. CEO Joann Björnson, COO Ron Reed and Director of Programs Laura Giorgi are now verified.
- Social handles were "unverified." Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn are now verified; YouTube, X and TikTok were not found.
