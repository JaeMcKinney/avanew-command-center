## 1. Company Overview

- Nightjar is a licensed, independent, **women-owned** cannabis retail brand serving **New Jersey and Connecticut**.
- Independently operated dispensary locations: **Bloomfield, NJ**, **East Lyme, CT**, and **Hamden, CT**.
- Dispensaries are designed and operated by real cannabis lovers; the team is dedicated to hospitality, transparency, and exceptional vibes.
- Serves first-time recreational explorers, seasoned consumers, and medical patients alike.
- General contact: **whatsgood@nightjarcannabis.com**

## 2. Locations

### Nightjar — Bloomfield, NJ
- Address: 549 Bloomfield Avenue, Bloomfield, NJ
- Phone: (973) 707-2915
- Hours: Mon–Sat 9 AM – 9 PM; Sun 11 AM – 7 PM
- Serves Bloomfield and nearby Montclair, Maplewood, East Orange, and surrounding Essex County towns.

### Nightjar — East Lyme, CT
- Address: 15 Colton Road, East Lyme, CT
- Directions: Off I-95 at Exit 71 (Four Mile River Rd), quick left onto Colton Rd.
- Serves the Connecticut shoreline including New London and Mystic.

### Nightjar — Hamden, CT
- Address: 1409 Dixwell Avenue, Hamden, CT 06514
- Phone: (203) 691-1707
- Hamden's first medical **and** adult-use dispensary; developed by Soulstar Holdings in partnership with Hamden native Joe Natale.
- On-site parking available.
- Serves Hamden, New Haven, North Haven, Woodbridge, and the SCSU community.

## 3. Products & Menu

- Categories: flower, pre-rolls, vapes, concentrates, edibles, topicals, drinks, accessories.
- Thoughtfully curated selection from diverse and independent brands, including: Garden Greens, Clade9, Miss Grass, Wyld, Fernway, Camino, ButACake, Wana, and more.
- House/featured brand collections include Nightjar and All Hours.
- Shopping: order online for quick in-store pickup, or shop in-store to browse the full selection. No shipping of cannabis products.
- Live menus per store:

  - https://nightjarcannabis.com/stores/nightjar-east-lyme-ct
  - https://nightjarcannabis.com/stores/nightjar-hamden-ct

## 4. Loyalty & Community

- Loyalty program: **Flight Club Rewards** — every purchase brings members closer to bigger discounts.
- Nightjar hosts in-store events and promos (e.g., 4/20 weekend events at the Bloomfield location).

## 5. Compliance Notes (for the PAL)

- Adults 21+ with valid government-issued ID (or registered medical patients).
- No medical claims, no dosage prescriptions; "start low and go slow" guidance only.
- No cross-state transport, no sales to minors, no consumption-and-driving guidance.
- Pricing/availability questions → direct to the live online menu.
